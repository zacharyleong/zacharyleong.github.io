## Features
- multiple importance sampling (MIS)
- next event estimation (NEE)
- microfacet BRDF + BTDF
- thin lens depth of field
- russian roulette
- BVH acceleration structure (SAH construction)
- quasi-monte carlo low-discrepancy sampling with Sobol sequences
- OpenImageDenoise (OIDN)
- per-triangle materials
